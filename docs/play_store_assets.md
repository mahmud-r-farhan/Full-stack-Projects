**💧 Take Control of Your Media with LiquidSync Gallery**

Cloud fatigue is real. Why upload your private moments to someone else's server just to view them on another device? **LiquidSync Gallery** is the premium, privacy-first alternative designed for users who value digital sovereignty, blazing-fast performance, and elegant design.

LiquidSync isn't just another gallery app; it’s your personal media ecosystem. Built with a stunning "Liquid Crystal" glassmorphism UI, it combines the fluid UX of top-tier gallery apps with powerful, local-only sharing technology.

**📡 KILLER FEATURE: LAN SHARE**
Experience a "Cloud-like" feeling without the Cloud. Start the built-in LAN server and instantly browse your phone's gallery from any laptop, tablet, or smart TV on your Wi-Fi network. 
- No internet required.
- No cables needed.
- No data limits.
- Just scan the QR code and go.

Your most sensitive media stays truly private. Move photos and videos to the Vault, secured by your device's biometrics (Fingerprint or Face ID).
- AES-256 encrypted storage for asset data.
- Hidden from the system MediaStore.
- One-tap lock for instant protection.

Stop waiting for thumbnails to load. LiquidSync is optimized for high-capacity galleries.
- Isolate-based thumbnail rendering for 60fps scrolling.
- Paginated loading prevents memory crashes.
- Performance Mode for older devices.

Inspired by Apple Photos UX and rendered with a beautiful glassmorphism aesthetic.
- Immersive backgrounds and smooth micro-animations.
- Material You support: The app theme adapts to your wallpaper (Android 12+).

**📱 KEY FEATURES:**
- **Zero Cloud Dependency:** Your data never leaves your device.

In an era of constant tracking, LiquidSync stands for privacy. We collect zero data. No analytics by default. No cloud sync. Just you and your memories.
